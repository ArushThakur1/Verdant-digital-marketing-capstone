# Verdant-digital-marketing-capstone
Digital marketing capstone project for Verdant, a sustainable sneaker brand, covering customer personas, marketing funnel, SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, Instagram, email and WhatsApp marketing strategies.
## Project Overview

This Digital Marketing Capstone project presents an integrated digital marketing strategy for Verdant, a sustainable sneaker brand focused on eco-friendly footwear for everyday India.

## Project Objectives

- Build awareness for Verdant and its sustainable sneaker offering
- Target eco-conscious consumers and young professionals
- Build trust through NFC-based sustainability verification
- Drive product consideration through customization and educational content
- Convert interested audiences into customers
- Develop an integrated digital marketing funnel

## Key Areas Covered

- Customer Persona Development
- Competitor Analysis
- Marketing Funnel Strategy
- SEO & Keyword Planning
- Google Ads Campaign
- Meta Ads Campaign
- Instagram Marketing
- Email Marketing
- WhatsApp Marketing
- Lead Generation & Nurturing
- Paid, Owned & Earned Media (POEM) Strategy

## Target Audience

The project focuses primarily on eco-conscious consumers, young professionals, and content creators interested in sustainable and stylish footwear.

## Digital Marketing Strategy

The strategy follows a full-funnel approach:

**TOFU – Awareness:** Build awareness through educational content, Instagram Reels, SEO blogs, and sustainability-focused content.

**MOFU – Consideration:** Build trust through product comparisons, NFC transparency demonstrations, customization content, and testimonials.

**BOFU – Conversion:** Drive purchases through product landing pages, promotional offers, customer reviews, bundles, and strong purchase CTAs.

## Campaign Channels

- Google Search Ads
- Meta Ads
- Instagram
- SEO & Content Marketing
- Email Marketing
- WhatsApp Marketing

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how multiple digital marketing channels can work together to create awareness, build consumer trust, generate leads, and drive conversions for a sustainable footwear brand.
